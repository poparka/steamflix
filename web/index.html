Settings

Where files come from

SteamFlix can pull blobs and dats over HTTP from the mirrors, out of the steam2 torrent, or from both. The mirrors are much faster per file; the swarm keeps working when they are down and spreads the load when you queue several titles at once.

Mirrors

Tried in order, top first. A mirror that errors is benched for a couple of minutes and traffic moves to the next one.

Traffic

These mirrors are run by volunteers. Everything here exists so SteamFlix can fetch a whole delta chain without behaving like a scraper - the defaults are already gentle, and turning them down further costs you nothing but time.

Proxies

Off by default. Useful if a mirror blocks your ISP; otherwise it only makes downloads slower and routes your traffic through machines run by strangers. Public lists are mostly dead entries, so everything is checked before it is used.

Diagnostics

Checks this installation end to end: Python, packages, the catalogue, disk space, the extractor, the key table, both mirrors, the torrent swarm and everything already downloaded.
